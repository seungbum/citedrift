\section{Demonstration}
\label{sec:demo}
At the venue we bring a machine with one consumer GPU (all measurements here are on a 12\,GB desktop RTX~5070) running the full system: the explorer works offline, check mode needs the venue network. Visitors can (1) browse the 2023$\rightarrow$2026 explorer, click an engine's ``content changed'' segment and read, side by side, the sentence an annotator copied in 2023 and what the page says today; (2) paste an answer they obtained from any AI search engine during the conference, optionally with a past date, and see each citation's outcome appear, with both judge scores and the passages that decided it, as its page is fetched; and (3) toggle the explainer to see why a citation was flagged.
