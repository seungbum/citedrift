CiteDrift

Checks every statement–citation pair of an AI search answer twice: against the page as it was when it was cited, and against the page today. Everything runs locally on one consumer GPU.

Table view

With a date, the Internet Archive snapshot closest to it is used as the "then" page. Without one, only today's support is judged. Pages are fetched once, on your request, by your own machine.
